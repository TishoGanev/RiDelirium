What it does

Your whole day on the mountain, kept.

Press start at the lift, ride, and RiDelirium works out the rest.

●

Live tracking

Speed, distance, max speed and altitude on a full-screen map, with your crew on it too. Follow Me keeps the map on you. Tracking keeps running with the screen off.

↓

Runs, lifts and stops

Every ride is split automatically into downhill runs, lift rides and time standing still. See each one, with distance, vertical and top speed.

▤

Season stats

Total distance, days on snow and top speed for the season, then every day in a list. Tap a day for the route.

▶

Replay

Watch your day play back along the map at 1×, 2×, 4×, 8× or 16×. Pause, restart, or drag to any moment.

↗

Share your ride

Make a ride card with your route and numbers. Post it to your Instagram or Facebook Story, or to your profile through the share sheet.

◎

Ride Together

Join friends with a QR code or an invite link and see your crew on the map while you ride, with speed or when they stopped. Tap a rider to send “Wait for me”. Works across iPhone and Android.

Questions

FAQ

Does it work without signal?

Yes. GPS tracking and recording don’t need a data connection, so rides are saved even high on the mountain. Map images, place names and seeing your crew do need a connection.

Will it drain my battery?

GPS tracking uses power, more so in the cold. For a full day, start with a charged phone, keep it in an inner pocket, and use low power mode if you like. Tracking only runs while you are recording a ride.

How does it know a lift from a run?

It looks at your altitude and speed. Going down at speed is a run, going up is a lift, and barely moving is a stop. A quick pause or a flat stretch doesn’t split a run in two. Very short or tiny segments are folded into their neighbours.

Where is my data stored?

Your rides are stored on your phone. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is collected, what leaves your device, and how to delete it.

Which devices does it need?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later (Apple Watch is optional), or an Android phone with Android 12 or later.
