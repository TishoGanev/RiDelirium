Delete your RiDelirium data

How to remove your data from the RiDelirium app (iPhone and Android) and from our server.

The quick way: open RiDelirium, go to Settings and tap DELETE MY RIDE TOGETHER DATA. Everything we hold about you on our server is deleted at once.

Steps in the app

  1. Open RiDelirium on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Open Settings (the gear button).
  3. Tap DELETE MY RIDE TOGETHER DATA and confirm.

The app removes you from every crew, tells the other riders you left, deletes your data from our server and forgets your name, ID and crews on your phone.

Can’t use the app? Ask us by email

Email support [at] ridelirium.app from any address with the subject “Delete my RiDelirium data”. Include the display name you used in the app and, if you remember it, the name of a crew you were in, so we can find your record. We delete it and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your display name, initials and the random ID of your install.
  • Your invite code, your crew memberships and your friend links.
  • Wait requests you sent or received and the list of riders you recently rode with.
  • Your push notification token and device records.
  • Your latest shared position and speed, if you were sharing one.

What is kept

  • Your rides. Ride history, routes, stats and place names are stored only on your phone and were never sent to us. Delete a ride by swiping it left in Stats, or uninstall the app to remove all of them. CSV files and images you exported or shared are copies that live where you saved them.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashed, a report went to Google’s Firebase Crashlytics. It contains no name, location or ride data and is not linked to your profile. Google deletes reports automatically after 90 days.

Automatic deletion

  • Your shared position is deleted ten minutes after your phone last sent it.
  • A rider who has not used the app for 30 days is deleted automatically, even if the app was uninstalled without using the button.
  • A crew with no members left is deleted.
