Support

Help with tracking, permissions, sharing and your data.

Need a hand? Email support [at] ridelirium.app. Tell us your phone model, OS version and what happened, and we’ll get back to you.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and allow location access when asked.
  2. Wait until the START TRACKING button shows a GPS reading, for example GPS ±4 M.
  3. Tap START TRACKING at the lift or the top of the first run.
  4. Tap STOP when you’re done. The ride appears in Stats.

Common questions

My ride didn’t record, or stopped when the screen turned off

  • Check location permission. On iPhone choose Always (or While Using) for RiDelirium in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. On Android allow location and notifications for the app.
  • On Android, don’t swipe the app away or restrict its battery use while recording. Some phones stop apps aggressively; set RiDelirium to “Unrestricted” in battery settings.
  • Wait for a GPS lock before you start. Under thick cloud or in a gondola the signal can be weak.

The numbers look off (distance, speed, altitude)

GPS is less accurate with a weak signal, and altitude from GPS can drift by several metres. The app ignores readings with poor accuracy and doesn’t count GPS drift while you stand still. “WEAK GPS” on the START TRACKING button means accuracy is low right now.

Runs and lifts are labelled wrongly

Runs, lifts and stops are worked out from your altitude and speed. A very short run, a long flat traverse, or a slow T-bar can be labelled differently than you’d expect. Send us the date and what you expected and we’ll use it to improve the detection.

Battery drains quickly

GPS and the screen are the main cost, and cold makes batteries weaker. Keep your phone warm in an inside pocket, dim the screen, and carry a small power bank for long days.

Sharing a ride

  • Tap Share on a ride. The share sheet opens with a ride card (route, distance and top speed) and a short text; pick any app.
  • To post on Instagram, choose Save Image and post it from Instagram; its share sheet entry may only offer messages.

Ride Together

  • Tap RIDE WITH SOMEONE at the top of the Track screen. Show your QR code or send your invite link, or scan a friend’s code to join their crew.
  • Friends in your crew appear on your map while the app is open. YOUR CREW on the Track card shows how many are online and offline.
  • Tap a rider on the map and press WAIT to ask them to wait for you. Allow notifications so you get their answer.
  • You need a data connection to see your crew. If the mountain signal drops, they reappear when it returns.

Export my rides

Open a ride in Stats and tap Export. You get a CSV file with time, latitude, longitude, altitude and speed for every point.

Delete a ride or all my data

Swipe a ride left in Stats and confirm to delete it. Uninstalling the app removes all rides. To remove your Ride Together profile from our server, see Delete your data. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Apple Watch and Apple Health (iPhone)

When you track on your phone, the watch app starts a workout for the sport you picked in Settings (snowboarding, skiing, cycling or hiking) and shows your heart rate. Allow Health access when asked. If the Apple Health line doesn’t show on a ride’s page, the ride was recorded without the watch or Health access wasn’t allowed (Settings → Health → Data Access).

Requirements

  • iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch is optional.
  • Android phone with Android 7.0 or later and Google Play services.
