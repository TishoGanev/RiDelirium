Support

Help with tracking, permissions, sharing and your data.

Need a hand? Email support@yourdomain.com. Tell us your phone model, OS version and what happened, and we’ll get back to you.

Getting started

  1. Open the app and allow location access when asked.
  2. Wait for GPS LOCKED on the Track card.
  3. Tap START TRACKING at the lift or the top of the first run.
  4. Tap STOP when you’re done. The ride appears in Stats.

Common questions

My ride didn’t record, or stopped when the screen turned off

  • Check location permission. On iPhone choose Always (or While Using) for Snowboarder in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. On Android allow location and notifications for the app.
  • On Android, don’t swipe the app away or restrict its battery use while recording. Some phones stop apps aggressively; set Snowboarder to “Unrestricted” in battery settings.
  • Wait for a GPS lock before you start. Under thick cloud or in a gondola the signal can be weak.

The numbers look off (distance, speed, altitude)

GPS is less accurate with a weak signal, and altitude from GPS can drift by several metres. The app ignores readings with poor accuracy and doesn’t count GPS drift while you stand still. “WEAK GPS” on the Track card means accuracy is low right now.

Runs and lifts are labelled wrongly

Runs, lifts and stops are worked out from your altitude and speed. A very short run, a long flat traverse, or a slow T-bar can be labelled differently than you’d expect. Send us the date and what you expected and we’ll use it to improve the detection.

Battery drains quickly

GPS and the screen are the main cost, and cold makes batteries weaker. Keep your phone warm in an inside pocket, dim the screen, and carry a small power bank for long days.

Sharing to Instagram or Facebook

  • Story: tap Share on a ride and pick Instagram Story or Facebook Story. These options only show up if the app is installed.
  • Your profile or feed: pick “Post to my profile…” (iPhone) or “More…” (Android) and choose the app in the share sheet. Instagram’s share sheet entry may only offer messages; use Save Image and post it from Instagram.

Group voice

  • Tap the microphone button on the Track screen. Everyone who uses the same group code is in one call. Share your code from the same screen, or type a friend’s code to join theirs.
  • Allow microphone access when asked. On Android also allow notifications, so the call keeps running with the screen off.
  • Use Open mic with a headset or helmet speaker, or Push to talk in wind. Wear a headset: don’t hold the phone while you ride.
  • You need a data connection for the call. If the mountain signal drops, rejoin when it returns.

Export my rides

Open a ride in Stats and tap Export. You get a CSV file with time, latitude, longitude, altitude and speed for every point.

Delete a ride or all my data

Swipe a ride left in Stats and confirm to delete it. Uninstalling the app removes all rides. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Apple Watch and Apple Health (iPhone)

When you track on your phone, the watch app starts a snowboarding workout and shows your heart rate. Allow Health access when asked. If the Apple Health line doesn’t show on a ride’s page, the ride was recorded without the watch or Health access wasn’t allowed (Settings → Health → Data Access).

Requirements

  • Android phone with Android 12 or later and Google Play services.
