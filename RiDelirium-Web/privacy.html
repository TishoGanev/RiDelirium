Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

RiDelirium (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is an independent app. This policy explains what the app does with your information on iPhone, Apple Watch and Android.

The short version

  • Your rides are recorded and stored on your phone. Our server never receives your routes. If you ride with friends, it keeps your name, who you ride with and, your latest position while you ride with friends (the app is open and you are in a crew, or you are recording a ride), so they can see you on their map.
  • We don’t sell your data.
  • A few features send limited data to other companies to work (maps, place names, sharing), and crash reports go to Google’s Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix bugs. They are listed below.

Location permission

The app asks for location access. You can choose “While using the app” or “Always” on iPhone, or “While using the app” on Android. Recording a ride in the background relies on that permission, and on Android on a notification that says a ride is being recorded. You can change the permission in your phone’s settings at any time. Without it, the app can’t record rides.

Other companies involved

  • Apple — Apple Maps and location services, geocoding, Apple Health and Apple Watch. apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google — Google Maps, Google Play services location and geocoding on Android. policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash reports on iPhone and Android. firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram) — only if you choose to share a ride card to Instagram or Facebook. Meta’s own policies apply from that point. facebook.com/privacy/policy

Sharing a ride

When you tap Share, the app creates an image of your route, distance and top speed on your phone and hands it to the app you pick. We don’t see it. What you post and who sees it is controlled by that app and your settings in it. The image shows the route you rode, so check that before posting if you don’t want to show where you ride or start.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete a single ride: swipe it left in Stats and confirm.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app. Rides are stored only inside the app.
  • CSV files you export, and images you share, are copies that live where you saved or posted them. Delete those yourself.
  • Heart rate, workouts and downhill distance in Apple Health are managed in the Health app.
  • Crash reports are deleted by Firebase Crashlytics automatically after 90 days.
  • Ride Together: in Settings, tap Delete my Ride Together data to remove your name, ID and crews from the server and from your phone at once (iPhone and Android). You can also leave a crew in the app, or email us and we will delete your name, ID and friend links from the server. Uninstalling the app does not remove the server record, which is deleted automatically after 30 days without use.

Children

The app isn’t directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Ride data stays inside the app’s private storage on your phone and is protected by your device’s own security and backup settings. Requests to our friends server use an encrypted connection. No method is perfectly secure, but we keep what leaves your device to a minimum.

Your rights

Because your rides stay on your phone, you control them directly with the steps above. If you live somewhere with data protection laws such as the GDPR or CCPA and want to ask about anything we hold, write to us at the address below.

Changes

If the app starts doing something new with your data, we will update this page and the date at the top before that feature ships.
